# Code of conduct

Be respectful and inclusive. We are all here to build something useful together.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery.
- Trolling, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@dlepaux](https://github.com/dlepaux), at the email address on their GitHub profile. Every report is
reviewed promptly and fairly, and kept confidential.
